Chapter 1

1What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency (or “crypto”) is simply money that lives on the internet. You can't hold it in your hand like a coin or a note, but it is real, it has value, and you can send it to anyone in the world in seconds.

If you use M-Pesa, you already understand most of it. M-Pesa is money on your phone. Crypto is the same idea — money you keep and send from your phone — but with two big differences:

  • No single company or government owns it. M-Pesa is run by Safaricom. Crypto is run by thousands of computers around the world working together, so no one person can freeze it or print more of it to make it worthless.
  • It works everywhere. You can send crypto from Nairobi to New York in seconds, any time of day or night, without a bank.

How is it kept safe?

Every crypto payment is written into a giant shared notebook called the blockchain. Thousands of computers all keep the same copy of this notebook, so nobody can cheat, fake a payment, or erase your money. This is why crypto is trusted by millions of people.

In one sentence Cryptocurrency is digital money you fully own on your phone — fast, global, and not controlled by any bank or government.

There are thousands of cryptocurrencies, but you only need to know a few. Here are the big ones:

Ξ

There are many more coins for different uses — faster payments, cheaper fees, new projects. As a beginner you don't need them. Start with the ones above.

4What is P2P trading?

P2P means “peer-to-peer” — person to person. Instead of buying crypto from a shop or a bank, you buy and sell it directly with other ordinary people.

It happens on a marketplace like Binance P2P. Think of it as a busy market:

  • Some people want to buy USDT and will pay you by M-Pesa or bank transfer.
  • Some people want to sell USDT for cash.
  • You stand in the middle and connect them — and earn a little on every deal.

How a single trade works

  • A buyer picks your offer and sends you M-Pesa.
  • You check the money has truly arrived.
  • You release the USDT to them.
  • Done — that's one trade.
You are the shopkeeper In P2P you act like a little money shop: people come to you to swap cash and crypto, and you take a small margin each time. Do it many times and it adds up.

5How people make money

The secret is simple: buy a little cheaper, sell a little higher. The small difference is your profit. Traders call it the “spread.”

A real example

You buy 1,000 USDT at 129 KES each → you pay 129,000 KES.

You sell those 1,000 USDT at 130 KES each → you receive 130,000 KES.

Profit: 1,000 KES — from just one trade.

One trade may look small. But P2P is about doing it again and again. The real earners win on volume:

  • A profit of 1,000 KES on one trade…
  • …done 20–40 times a day…
  • …becomes a serious daily income.

Becoming a “merchant”

When you trade a lot and treat buyers well, you can become a merchant — a trusted seller buyers come to first. More trades, steadier income, a real business run from your phone.

The honest truth The money is real — but it depends on speed, trust, and never missing a trade. That is exactly where most people struggle… and exactly where SparkP2P comes in.
